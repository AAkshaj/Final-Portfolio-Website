Major Project 1: Career Materials

For Major Project 1, I created a professional resume and cover letter tailored toward a software engineering internship opportunity. The goal of this assignment was to practice communicating technical experience clearly for a professional audience, especially recruiters and hiring managers who need to quickly understand my skills, impact, and fit for a role. This project illustrates my growth as a technical communicator because I learned how to move beyond simply listing tasks and instead frame my experience around measurable results, relevant technologies, and audience expectations. Through revision, I strengthened the clarity, organization, and persuasiveness of my career materials so that they better represented both my technical background and professional goals.
