Major Project 2: Technical Crisis Report and Infographic

For Major Project 2, I created a technical crisis report and accompanying infographic analyzing how organizations communicated during controversy surrounding the reported use of Anthropic’s Claude AI in a U.S. military operation. The written report allowed me to examine stakeholder messaging, rhetorical framing, and the broader implications of transparency and accountability in AI-enabled operations, while the infographic translated those findings into a more visual and accessible format for broader audiences. This project demonstrates my ability to communicate complex technical and ethical issues across multiple modes while adapting depth and presentation to audience needs. It also reflects my growth in organization and design, as I revised the report for stronger structure and transformed dense research into visual communication that outside audiences could quickly understand.
