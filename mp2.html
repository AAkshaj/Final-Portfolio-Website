Major Project 2: Technical Crisis Report and Infographic

For Major Project 2, I created a technical crisis report and an accompanying infographic that examined how organizations communicate during moments of controversy or public concern. The written report allowed me to analyze the issue in depth by explaining context, stakeholders, evidence, and communication strategies, while the infographic translated the same topic into a more visual and accessible format. This project demonstrates my ability to communicate complex technical and ethical information across different modes. It also reflects my growth in organization and design, since I had to revise the report for clearer structure and adapt dense research into a format that outside audiences could quickly understand.
