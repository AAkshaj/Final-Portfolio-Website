Major Project 3: Community Training Materials

For Major Project 3, I created community training materials designed to help users learn a technical tool through clear, accessible instruction. This assignment focused on making information useful for a real audience by combining written explanation, step-by-step guidance, visual organization, and practical examples. My project demonstrates my growth as a technical communicator because it required me to think carefully about usability, audience needs, and how people learn unfamiliar systems. Rather than presenting information all at once, I organized the materials into a structured learning experience that reduces confusion and supports users as they build confidence with the tool.
