Major Project 3: Community Training Materials

For Major Project 3, I created community training materials and a recommendation report focused on improving Bloomberg Terminal onboarding for Georgia Tech students. The project involved identifying communication barriers within Bloomberg Terminal’s steep learning curve and designing instructional materials that would make the platform more accessible to novice users. My final deliverables combined written explanation, step-by-step workflows, visual organization, and practical examples to create a structured learning experience. This project demonstrates my growth as a technical communicator because it required me to prioritize usability, audience needs, and instructional clarity while designing materials for real users. Rather than simply presenting information, I focused on creating an intuitive training system that reduces confusion and supports progressive learning.
